Delivery, Refunds & Returns

What you pay for, when it arrives, and what happens if something isn't right.

Last updated: October 2026

This page covers how Amabile di Rosa ("we", "us", "our") delivers your order and how we handle refunds and returns. It applies to orders placed and paid for through amabiledirosa.com. If anything here is unclear, email us at trade@amabiledirosa.com and we'll help.

Delivery timeline

  • We deliver within 2 to 4 business days of your payment being confirmed, within our active delivery areas in Nigeria.
  • We'll contact you on the phone number or email you provide to arrange the drop-off and confirm the address.
  • Delivery times can be affected by things outside our control, such as weather, traffic or public holidays. If your order will be delayed, we'll let you know.

Delivery cost and areas

Any delivery fee is shown at checkout before you pay. We currently deliver within selected commercial areas in Nigeria. If we can't deliver to your address, we'll tell you before taking payment or refund you in full.

Receiving your order

Our wine is for adults of legal drinking age (18 and over). The person receiving the order must be 18 or older, and we may ask for ID on delivery. If no one of legal drinking age is available to receive it, we may not be able to complete the delivery.

Refunds and returns

We want you to be happy with your order. Here's how we handle it:

  • Damaged, wrong or faulty on arrival: if your order arrives damaged, incomplete, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and your order details. We'll arrange a replacement or a full refund, including any delivery fee.
  • Cancelling before dispatch: if you need to cancel, reach us before your order has been dispatched and we'll refund you in full.
  • Change of mind on unopened items: because we sell alcohol, we can only accept returns of bottles that are unopened, undamaged and in their original condition, reported within 7 days of delivery. Return delivery may be at your cost unless the item was faulty.
  • Opened bottles: for health, safety and regulatory reasons, opened bottles cannot be returned or refunded unless they were faulty.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method. Once we've confirmed the refund, it is typically processed within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

How to request a refund or return

Email trade@amabiledirosa.com with your order details and, where relevant, a photo of the issue. We aim to respond within 2 business days and will guide you through the next steps.

Prefer to talk it through? Reach us at trade@amabiledirosa.com and we'll sort it out.
