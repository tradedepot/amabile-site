Andiamo!By Amabile di Rosa

Privacy

What we keep about you, why we keep it, and how to have it removed.

Updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Andiamo! is a game by Amabile di Rosa, the Italian wine brand. Andiamo! lets you send an Amabile to a friend at any spot on the map, and find free bottle drops and other cool stuff around your city.

Questions about your data go to hello@amabiledirosa.com.

What we keep

  • Your WhatsApp number. It's your account. We send your sign-in code to it.
  • Your name and the look you pick for your character.
  • The bottles you send and receive: who sent it, who it's for, the note, the wine, the venue and whether it was served. If you send one anonymously, your friend doesn't see your name, but we still keep it.
  • Payments. Paystack takes your card or bank details, and we never see them. We keep the amount and Paystack's reference.
  • Messages you send to the Andiamo! WhatsApp number. Our team reads and answers them. We also keep the name on your WhatsApp profile, so we can fill in your first name for you.

Your location

We only ask for it when you claim a bottle, switch your light on or join a drop. We use it at that moment to work out which venue you're at. We keep the venue and your table, not where you were.

If there's no spot on the map near you, we keep a rough location, to about 100 metres, without your number. It tells us where to add venues next.

Your light and your friends

When your light is on, friends in your circle can see which venue you're at. It goes off after three hours, and always at 2am. You choose who's in your circle, and you can hide from or block anyone.

If you pick friends from your phone's contacts, each number is scrambled on your phone before it reaches us. We only use them to show you which of those friends are playing, now or when they join.

When you claim a bottle, the venue sees your name and table so they can bring it to you.

WhatsApp messages from us

We message you for two things only: your sign-in code, and to tell you a bottle is waiting for you. We don't send marketing. If you'd rather not get bottle messages, tell us and we'll stop them.

Venues and their staff

For our venues we keep the business contact, the bank account we pay into, which Paystack checks with the bank, and the names and WhatsApp numbers of the staff who sign in to the runner board.

Who else handles it

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the services that run Andiamo!:

  • Meta, for WhatsApp codes and messages.
  • Paystack, for payments and paying venues.
  • Netlify, where Andiamo! runs and your data is stored.
  • Slack, where our team reads messages sent to the Andiamo! number.
  • Google Maps, only if you tap Directions.

We also share it where the law requires us to.

On your phone

Andiamo! saves a few things in your browser to keep you signed in and remember your settings. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. Lights go off after three hours. Bottles and payments are kept for as long as we need them to run the game and keep proper accounts. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email hello@amabiledirosa.com or message the Andiamo! WhatsApp number. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

18 and over

Andiamo! is for people 18 and over. If we learn a number belongs to someone younger, we stop it from playing.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
