What Andiamo is

Andiamo is Amabile's Depletion Programme, to help on-trade venues deplete their stock.

1List Amabile
2Join Andiamo!

How it works

People send a bottle of Amabile to a friend's table at any of our venues, from their phone.

  1. 1
    Someone sends a bottleThey pay on their phone, to a friend's number or a table in the room.
  2. 2
    Their friend goes outTo any venue on Andiamo, and asks the waiter for it.
  3. 3
    The venue serves itFrom its own stock. We pay its full menu price, immediately.

Your part: get our venues on Andiamo. Let's deplete for them.

At the venue

  1. 1

    Talk to the manager

    Use the lines in What to say, word for word. Lead with the money: full menu price, paid immediately.

  2. 2

    Add the store on your phone

    andiamo.ng/onboard › Add a store. About three minutes.

  3. 3

    Approved at the back end, it's live

    You and the manager both get a WhatsApp.

  4. 4

    Set up the bar phone

    andiamo.ng/board on the phone that stays at the bar. The manager signs in once, then taps Test the sound.

  5. 5

    Walk the staff through one bottle

    1Board pings
    2I'm on it
    3Find the glowing phone
    4Scan their QR or type the 4 digits
    5Delivered

    Paid the moment it's Delivered.

  6. 6

    Run one real bottle

    A test bottle is sent to your number. Claim it at a table with the staff watching.
